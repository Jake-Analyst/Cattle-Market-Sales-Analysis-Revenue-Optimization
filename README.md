# Cattle Market Sales Analysis & Revenue Optimization (2024–2026)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes cattle market sales data from 2024 to 2026 to identify revenue trends, evaluate breed performance, compare market contributions, and uncover opportunities for revenue optimization. The goal is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and interactive visualization.

## Business Problem

The original dataset contained missing numeric values, inconsistent date ordering, and no month or day metadata. These issues limited accurate seasonal analysis and made it difficult to identify the main drivers of sales revenue.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data cleaning, missing value imputation, date standardization, and feature engineering.
- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and analysis.
- **Excel:** Initial data profiling and exploration.
- **Tableau:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

## Data Preparation & Analysis

- Inspected the dataset and identified data quality issues.
- Handled missing numeric values.
- Standardized date formats and chronological ordering.
- Extracted month and day features to support seasonal analysis.
- Analyzed revenue by month, cattle breed, market, and grade.
- Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to communicate key findings.

## Key Findings

- **Peak Revenue Month:** May recorded the highest revenue.
- **Top-Performing Breeds:** Male Zebu and mixed breeds were the leading performers.
- **Top Revenue Market:** Katito Market contributed the highest revenue.
- **Highest Sales Volume:** Grade 2 recorded the highest volume.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing breeds to strengthen revenue generation.
- Focus sales efforts on Katito Market, given its strong revenue contribution.
- Develop targeted strategies for low-revenue months, such as August.
- Use seasonal trends and market performance to improve sales planning and resource allocation.
